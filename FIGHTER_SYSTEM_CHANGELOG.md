## 25/11/2026 — Reson: Dissonance buffed
- Dissonance now hurts as well as scrambles movement: damage 24 → 55 (description updated). Still no push or stun, same 3.2 m radius, 10 s cooldown and 22 stamina. Reason: owner asked for a buff that makes it deal real damage. The balance harness test still passes; no full matrix was run.

## 25/11/2026 — Psion: Telekinetic Toss lift time follows weight
- The rise before the throw is now `0.3 s + 0.09 s × √mass`, capped at 2.6 s (a chair about 0.6 s, a 160 kg stone block about 1.4 s, the heaviest ruins 2.6 s). Previously every object rose in a flat 0.55 s. Heavier objects still hit harder. Description updated. Provisional values.

## 25/11/2026 — Reson: Vibration Punch field
- Vibration Punch now shows a field of rippling air held at the punching fist: a quivering halo through the wind-up, then a cone of rolling distortion waves, echoes and sound lines reaching out to the blow's full reach as the fist drives in, fading over the recovery. It is drawn from the arm every frame (never a separate projectile) and the hit is still the same instant strike.
- Reach 1.9 → 2.4 m (aim range 2 → 2.4 m), so he lands it from a little further than normal melee. Damage (117), cooldown (7 s), stamina (28) and knockback are unchanged.

## 25/11/2026 — Psion: Hypnosis replaces Mind Crush; Necro: Soul Drain look
- **Psion, Hypnosis (Q, replaces Mind Crush):** a spinning white spiral flies at the opponent (9 m, no damage of its own). On contact the opponent is entranced for 1.1 s while a weaker copy of them rises from them and steps out; the Psion's side then controls the copy (the player, or the AI if an AI Psion cast it) against the original while the Psion stands outside the fight and cannot be hurt. The copy looks like the original, has all its abilities except the ultimate, 40% of its health and 50% of its damage, and lasts 9 s. It ends early if the copy or the original falls (not a match result: the Psion's own health decides a time-limit match). When it ends, the original snaps out of the trance on the spot and Psion steps back in exactly where he stood when he cast it, not where the copy fell. One copy at a time; not in tag fights. Stamina 35, cooldown 30 s. Provisional values.
- **Necro, Soul Drain:** the bolt is grey with a subtle red glow at its centre. When it lands, small particles (more for more healing) are pulled out of the victim and curve back to Necro, each arriving with a faint red pulse. Damage (56), healing (15% of damage dealt), blind (3 s) and cooldown (4.5 s) are unchanged.

## 25/11/2026 — Ink Serpent wrap and Ink Bull polish (visuals only)
- **Ink Serpent:** the coil now winds in about four visible loops from shoulder to ankles, and the body is drawn on both sides of the opponent's own figure in the depth sort: the loops on the far side pass behind them and the loops on the near side cross in front, alternating all the way down. It follows the opponent as they walk for the whole 4 s hold. Mechanics unchanged.
- **Ink Bull:** drawn about 16% smaller (hit size, damage and charge unchanged). Smoother, rounder body and pool outlines; a galloping stride with a slow planted foot and a quick lifted swing, body bob and pitch, and a spine that bends into turns (shoulders lean in, haunch swings out). Mechanics unchanged.

## 25/11/2026 — Psion Gravity Vortex replaces Hypnosis; Ink Spear replaces Ink Cyclone; Bull ink splat; Serpent no running
- **Psion, Gravity Vortex (Q) replaces Hypnosis** (the hypnosis feature and its veil, copy and hand-over code were removed): a miniature gravity well opens up to 5 m ahead for 2.4 s, hurts a little each 0.5 s (8) and drags everyone inside hard toward its centre (3.4 m/s), then collapses in a burst (62 damage, 11 m/s launch outward, 3.2 m). Stamina 30, cooldown 13 s. Drawn in violet; reuses the vortex simulation Inkmaster's cyclone used.
- **Inkmaster, Ink Spear (fifth ability) replaces Ink Cyclone:** the brush grows into a long spear for 8 s; taps become a two-thrust poke (30, 32), a driving lunge (46) and the heavy becomes an impaling stab (62), all long (2.3-2.7 m), narrow and contact-only. Stamina 24, cooldown 16 s. New spear drawing, thrust animations and sounds.
- **Ink Bull:** a ram that lands splashes ink over the middle of the target's screen for 1.5 s.
- **Ink Serpent:** a held target can no longer run (as well as no attacks, abilities, jumps or dodges); walking and guarding stay free; it lasts exactly the 4 s hold and ends when the snake releases.

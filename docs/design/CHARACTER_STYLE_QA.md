# Character style QA checklist

**Owner, 12/11/2026:** "do quality check on the styling ensuring it meets the required standards based on the reference images."

The standard comes from the owner's reference art (flat, polished stickman fighters). Every change to a character, summon, weapon or held item must pass **every** item below before it is called done.

## How to check

1. Take screenshots in a real browser:
   - at gameplay zoom (1280×720), at 3× zoom on the character, and in slow motion (`?slowmo=0.12`) for any animation;
   - in the idle, walk, attack and ability poses;
   - facing both left and right.
2. Compare them side by side with the reference images and tick each item below.
3. If any item fails, fix it and take the screenshots again. Never report a change as done while an item fails.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Clean silhouette** | The shape reads at a glance at gameplay zoom: no scribbled, spiky or noisy outlines, no stray lines, no gaps between parts. |
| S2 | **Minimal inner detail** | At most two or three bold inner marks per part (a highlight band, a seam, a gap). No rows of small dots, knobs, teeth or hatching. |
| S3 | **Flat colour, two-tone cel shading** | One base colour per part, one lit band toward the light (up and to the left on screen) and one shadow band. No speckle or busy texture. |
| S4 | **Coloured edges** | Edges are a deep shade of the part's own colour, at the figure's outline width. No black ink lines inside the body. |
| S5 | **Tapered forms** | Limbs, bones, weapons and staffs taper (thick to thin). No uniform sticks. Joints are rounded, not knobbly. |
| S6 | **Bold, simple props** | Weapons and staffs are a few strong shapes with one highlight. The prop's top or blade is the focal point and reads at gameplay zoom. |
| S7 | **Contained glow** | Glows and magic are soft and round, sit on the prop or body, and never hide the character. |
| S8 | **No eyes on stickmen** | No stickman has eyes (owner, 12/11/2026). Only the skull on Necro's staff has eye sockets, which glow while he casts. |
| S9 | **Consistent with the roster** | The same edge width, shading and proportions as Inferno, Braxon and the Dummy. Sizes are unchanged unless asked. |
| S10 | **Both facings, all poses** | Nothing breaks, detaches or flips wrongly when the character turns or animates. |
